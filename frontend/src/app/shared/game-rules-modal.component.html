Move all 4 of your marbles from their starting boxes, around the board, and into your home lane. First player to fill all 4 home slots wins.

🎲How to Play

  1. Each player receives 13 cards across 3 rounds (5 · 4 · 4). The deck holds 54 cards (incl. 2 Jokers); 2 stay in reserve each cycle.
  2. On your turn, play one card. You have 60 s — a move is auto-played when time runs out.
  3. Play A, K or a Joker to enter a marble onto your start position.
  4. Landing on an opponent's marble sends it back to its starting box.
  5. Once past your start position, you enter the home lane — land exactly on a free slot.
  6. No legal move? Discard your hand and wait for the next deal.

⚠️Key Rules

  • Freshly entered marble: A marble that has just entered the board on its start square (via an Ace or a King) is fully protected — it blocks passage, cannot be passed, captured, swapped, or moved backward. Once it moves forward, all protections are lifted, even if it later returns to the start square.
  • Jack restriction: Cannot swap with a marble already in a home lane.

Two teams of two: Red & Blue (diamond badges) vs Green & Orange (round badges), sitting across from each other. Your team wins when both partners have all 4 marbles in their home lane.

🤝Playing as a Team

  1. All 4 of your marbles home? You keep playing: your cards now control your teammate's marbles — entering them on their start square and promoting them into their home lane.
  2. Seven: move one of your own marbles first — the remaining steps may move one of your teammate's marbles.
  3. Jack: swap any two marbles of different colors — your own marble doesn't have to be involved.
  4. Blocked-hand rescue: if you have no legal move but hold an Ace, King or Joker, your teammate still has marbles in reserve and their start square is completely free, you must play that card to bring their marble in — discarding is not allowed.

⚠️What Doesn't Change

  • No team immunity: landing on your teammate's marble captures it and sends it back to its starting box, just like an opponent's.
  • Freshly entered marble: still blocks everyone — including your teammate — and stays protected from the Jack until it moves.
